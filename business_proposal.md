# Business Proposal: CRM Migration for Apex Cloud Services

**Prepared for:** CEO and Board of Directors  
**Project:** CRM migration and sales-data improvement  
**Planning period:** Six months

## 1\. Executive summary

Apex Cloud Services has 150 active sales representatives and currently tracks client information in offline spreadsheets. This creates fragmented records and makes consistent reporting difficult. This proposal recommends moving to a shared CRM platform and uses the assignment's supplied pricing assumptions to compare Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho CRM.

Under the assignment prices, HubSpot's three-year TCO is $496,000, compared with $835,000 for Salesforce and $221,000 for Zoho. Zoho is the lowest-cost option. In the illustrative weighted decision matrix, HubSpot scores 8.20/10 and Zoho scores 8.15/10. Therefore, HubSpot is retained as the planning recommendation because the assumed scores give it an advantage in setup speed and ease of adoption.

The score difference is small and the feature scores are preliminary judgments, not test results. Before purchase, Apex should verify current licensing, required features, integrations, security, support, and written quotations. The proposed implementation lasts six months.

## 2\. Business problem

The current spreadsheet-based approach can lead to:

* Duplicate or inconsistent client records.
* Fragmented customer history across teams.
* Manual reporting and limited visibility into the sales pipeline.
* Difficult access control and unclear data ownership.
* Extra work when preparing management reports.

The project will establish a shared system, standardized fields, agreed ownership, and consistent reporting practices.

## 3\. Objectives

1. Centralize sales and customer information in an approved CRM.
2. Improve data completeness and reduce duplicates.
3. Give sales managers a consistent view of pipeline activity.
4. Train the 150-person sales team and encourage sustained adoption.
5. Complete a controlled migration within six months, subject to stage-gate approval.

## 4\. Vendor comparison

The following cost figures use the assignment's supplied prices. They are assumptions for this exercise, not independently verified current quotations.

|Vendor|Assumed monthly price per user|Setup fee|1-year TCO|3-year TCO|
|-|-:|-:|-:|-:|
|Salesforce|$150|$25,000|$295,000|$835,000|
|HubSpot|$90|$10,000|$172,000|$496,000|
|Zoho CRM|$40|$5,000|$77,000|$221,000|

### Calculation method

* Annual recurring cost = 150 users × monthly price × 12.
* 1-year TCO = annual recurring cost + setup fee.
* 3-year TCO = 150 users × monthly price × 36 + setup fee.

**Salesforce**

* Annual recurring cost = 150 × $150 × 12 = $270,000.
* 1-year TCO = $270,000 + $25,000 = **$295,000**.
* Three-year recurring cost = 150 × $150 × 36 = $810,000.
* 3-year TCO = $810,000 + $25,000 = **$835,000**.

**HubSpot**

* Annual recurring cost = 150 × $90 × 12 = $162,000.
* 1-year TCO = $162,000 + $10,000 = **$172,000**.
* Three-year recurring cost = 150 × $90 × 36 = $486,000.
* 3-year TCO = $486,000 + $10,000 = **$496,000**.

**Zoho CRM**

* Annual recurring cost = 150 × $40 × 12 = $72,000.
* 1-year TCO = $72,000 + $5,000 = **$77,000**.
* Three-year recurring cost = 150 × $40 × 36 = $216,000.
* 3-year TCO = $216,000 + $5,000 = **$221,000**.

### Cost interpretation

* HubSpot is $123,000 less than Salesforce over one year and $339,000 less over three years.
* Zoho is $95,000 less than HubSpot over one year and $275,000 less over three years.
* These calculations include only subscription charges and the stated one-time setup fee. They exclude any costs not supplied in the assignment, such as extra modules, migration services, integrations, taxes, training, or support upgrades.

## 6\. Proposed solution and scope

The planning recommendation is to configure HubSpot CRM for Apex's agreed sales processes.

**In scope**

* Requirements and data inventory.
* Spreadsheet profiling, cleanup, and field mapping.
* CRM configuration, roles, permissions, and agreed reports.
* Pilot testing and feedback.
* Controlled data migration and reconciliation.
* Staff training, go-live support, and post-launch optimization.

**Out of scope unless separately approved**

* Unquoted third-party integrations or custom development.
* Additional software modules or licenses not in the assumptions.
* Major business-process redesign beyond agreed sales workflows.
* Ongoing managed services after project handover.

## 7\. Six-month implementation roadmap

|Phase|Timing|Main deliverables|
|-|-|-|
|Planning \& Data Cleanup|Month 1|Requirements, data inventory, deduplicated sample, field map, baseline KPIs.|
|System Configuration \& Pilot|Months 2–3|Configured CRM, pilot results, test evidence, approved migration method.|
|Full Migration \& Staff Training|Months 4–5|Migrated and reconciled records, training materials, trained users, go-live approval.|
|Post-Launch Optimization|Month 6|Adoption and data-quality review, issue closure, improvement backlog, handover.|

## 8\. Key risks and mitigations

|Risk|Probability|Impact|Mitigation|
|-|-|-|-|
|Poor-quality or duplicate data transfers into the new CRM.|High|High|Clean and map data, back up sources, test a sample migration, and reconcile records.|
|Sales staff do not consistently use the CRM.|Medium|High|Engage sales champions, provide role-based training, track adoption, and provide support.|
|Integration or permission errors affect service or data security.|Medium|High|Test integrations and access roles, use least privilege, review security, and document rollback procedures.|

## 9\. Governance and success measures

A project manager will coordinate work. Sales Operations will own business requirements and data quality; a CRM administrator will handle configuration; a data lead will manage migration; and sales managers will support training and adoption.

Establish Month 1 baselines and agree targets before launch. Track weekly active users, required-field completeness, duplicate rate, migration reconciliation, reporting time, and high-priority open issues.

## 10\. Recommendation and next steps

Proceed with HubSpot as the **planning scenario**, conditional on:

1. Confirming required functionality and licensing with the vendor.
2. Obtaining written quotations for all 150 users and all required setup services.
3. Reviewing security, privacy, access control, integrations, and data handling.
4. Running a pilot and validating the preliminary weighted scores.
5. Obtaining formal approval of scope, budget, success measures, and go-live criteria.

If verified requirements or quotations materially change the scores, reconsider Zoho or Salesforce before contract signature.
