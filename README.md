# Prompt Engineering Capstone: AI-Powered Business Automation \& Decision Systems

## Project overview

This capstone analyzes CRM migration options for Apex Cloud Services, a fictional case study with 150 sales representatives using offline spreadsheets.

## Objectives

* Use meta-prompting to generate a specialized CRM research prompt.
* Calculate 1-year and 3-year total cost of ownership (TCO).
* Create a weighted cost-benefit decision matrix.
* Prepare a six-month implementation plan and risk registry.
* Draft a business proposal, executive presentation, and lessons-learned report.

## Files in this repository

* `prompt\_portfolio.md` — prompts used across the capstone.
* `decision\_matrix.md` — weighted scoring calculations and recommendation.
* `business\_proposal.md` — full proposal for the CRM migration.
* `implementation\_plan.md` — six-month roadmap and risk registry.
* `executive\_presentation.md` — six-slide executive deck in Markdown.
* `lessons\_learned\_report.md` — reflections on AI use and verification.

## Key assumptions

The vendor prices are supplied by the course assignment and are not independently verified current quotations:

* Salesforce: $150/user/month + $25,000 setup.
* HubSpot: $90/user/month + $10,000 setup.
* Zoho CRM: $40/user/month + $5,000 setup.

The decision matrix's non-cost scores are illustrative judgments and should be validated by vendor demonstrations and a pilot.
