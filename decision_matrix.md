# Weighted Cost-Benefit Decision Matrix

## 1\. Criteria and weights

|Criterion|Weight|Reason|
|-|-:|-|
|TCO Cost Model|30%|The organization needs to control CRM spending.|
|Customization \& Scale|20%|The CRM should support business growth and workflows.|
|Setup Speed|25%|The organization wants to move away from fragmented spreadsheets.|
|User Adoption Ease|25%|The sales team must use the new system consistently.|
|**Total**|**100%**|0.30 + 0.20 + 0.25 + 0.25 = 1.00|

## 3\. Example calculation

HubSpot weighted score:

* TCO: 7 × 0.30 = 2.10
* Customization \& Scale: 8 × 0.20 = 1.60
* Setup Speed: 9 × 0.25 = 2.25
* User Adoption Ease: 9 × 0.25 = 2.25
* Total = 2.10 + 1.60 + 2.25 + 2.25 = **8.20 / 10**

Zoho weighted score:

* TCO: 10 × 0.30 = 3.00
* Customization \& Scale: 7 × 0.20 = 1.40
* Setup Speed: 8 × 0.25 = 2.00
* User Adoption Ease: 7 × 0.25 = 1.75
* Total = 3.00 + 1.40 + 2.00 + 1.75 = **8.15 / 10**

## 4\. Decision

Under these preliminary scores, **HubSpot ranks first at 8.20/10**, narrowly ahead of Zoho at 8.15/10. Zoho has the lowest assumed cost; HubSpot is selected in the assignment scenario because the illustrative scores favor setup speed and ease of adoption.

**Important:** The 0.05-point difference is small. The board should not treat it as proof of a definitive winner. Confirm required features, licensing, integrations, security, data migration needs, and actual vendor quotations before contracting.
